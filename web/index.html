        A Guardian PIN protects shared settings and approves restricted changes. Family Profiles requires at least one Guardian PIN to keep profile management in trusted hands.
        Add a Second Guardian PIN?
        Give another person you trust a Guardian PIN to approve protected actions and manage your shared shelf. Guardian access is separate from the Adult role and Adults group. GameShelf supports up to two Guardian PINs.

        This removes the PIN and all action restrictions. Everyone with access can use the shelf's controls again.
